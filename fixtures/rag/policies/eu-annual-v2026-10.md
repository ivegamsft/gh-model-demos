# Annual subscription refund policy

Document ID: REF-EU-ANNUAL
Version: 2026.10
Region: EU
Product: annual subscription
Effective from: 2026-10-01 (inclusive)
Last edited: 2026-10-12

## 1. Applicability

Applies to EU purchases only. It does not amend REF-US-ANNUAL.

## 2. Refund window

For this fictional product, a refund is allowed within 30 calendar days of an
initial annual purchase if no export jobs have completed. Renewals are excluded.
This fixture states a product policy, not a summary of any jurisdiction's law.
