## 1. Applicability

Applies only to US annual purchases made before 2026-10-01.
The later archive edit date does not extend applicability.

## 2. Initial purchase and renewal

Refunds are allowed within 30 calendar days of initial purchase or renewal,
regardless of export usage. No prorated refund is offered afterward.
