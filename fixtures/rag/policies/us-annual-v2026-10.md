# Annual subscription refund policy

Document ID: REF-US-ANNUAL
Version: 2026.10
Region: US
Product: annual subscription
Effective from: 2026-10-01 (inclusive)
Effective until: not superseded
Last edited: 2026-09-25
Supersedes: REF-US-ANNUAL version 2025.01

## 1. Applicability

This version applies to US annual subscriptions purchased on or after
2026-10-01. The purchase date, not the refund request date or document edit date,
selects the applicable version. Monthly plans and non-US purchases are excluded.

## 2. Initial purchase

A full refund is allowed if requested within 14 calendar days of the initial
purchase, provided no more than 10 export jobs have completed on the subscription.
Both conditions must hold. A request on day 14 qualifies; day 15 does not.

## 3. Renewal

A renewal is refundable within seven calendar days of the renewal charge only
if no export jobs have completed since that renewal. Otherwise it is not
refundable under this policy.

## 4. Exclusions and limits

After the applicable window, cancellation stops the next renewal but does not
create a prorated refund. This policy does not establish exceptions for
service outages, taxes, or third-party marketplace purchases.
