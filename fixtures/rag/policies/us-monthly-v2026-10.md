# Monthly subscription refund policy

Document ID: REF-US-MONTHLY
Version: 2026.10
Region: US
Product: monthly subscription
Effective from: 2026-10-01 (inclusive)
Last edited: 2026-10-13

## 1. Applicability

Monthly subscriptions only. Annual subscriptions are governed by REF-US-ANNUAL.

## 2. Refund window

The initial monthly charge is refundable within three calendar days if no
export jobs have completed. This document states no annual-plan refund rules.
